# 1.1 update, second batch (requested 2026-11-27)

Same update as the open/close work (not committed yet, both loaders). Everything lives in `common/` unless a loader hook is
unavoidable; every new hook goes through the masterball-api platform layer. All text: English in code/docs, en_us + es_es
(and es_mx etc. if the project already ships them) in lang files, generated by a `tools/gen_lang_12_<group>.py` script.
Art must be original (procedural/pixel art made by our scripts), never Pokémon/Create assets copied.

Config defaults that change must also reach existing worlds: bump the config's version field (or add one) and migrate
the old default value when the file still holds it.

## Group A: staff, security, register
1. **Cashiers serve properly.** Today they serve badly: find why (pathing, range, queue, timing) and fix. Cashiers walk up
   to waiting customers to serve them instead of waiting behind the counter only.
2. **Cashier upgrades** (phone upgrades): speed, range, accuracy, and a hard-to-get top upgrade "handles hagglers" (the
   cashier closes haggling customers itself at a fair result, so the owner doesn't have to).
3. **Guards:** at most 3 hired. Bug: after upgrading a guard it seems to stop defending; find and fix.
4. **Better upgrades UI:** clearer cards per staff member: level, what the next level gives, cost, one button.
5. **Register:** withdraw by percentage (25/50/75/100 and custom), a "Are you sure?" confirm, both on the register block
   UI and on the phone; also **deposit money into the register**.
6. **Tips:** no customer ever tips more than $50,000 (hard cap after every multiplier).
7. **Theft vs reputation:** the lower the reputation, the more likely a robbery; show the stats with clear bars in the
   Manager Goggles HUD (reputation, theft risk, cleanliness, decoration, staff).

## Group B: customers
1. **Haggling UI, simpler:** fewer choices, big clear buttons, one line saying what each does; readable for players
   with low attention.
2. **Greed punishment + charisma:** if the customer's first offer is already positive for you, asking for more (or
   offering them less) makes them leave. Instead give charisma options (a joke, a compliment, a story, small talk) whose
   success depends on Charisma, which raise the tip or mood. Design it so it's fun and not spammable.
3. **Market % vs reputation:** much harder for customers to accept prices above market; the accepted markup scales with
   reputation.
4. **200+ common Latin American names** in the NPC name pool (first names + surnames), mixed with existing ones.
5. **1000 more dialogue lines** across all customer types and situations, en + es.
6. **Wall/roof detection less strict:** a wall can have some gaps (windows, holes) and still count.
7. **No NPC may ever spawn on shop roofs.**
8. **Scalpers selling overpriced packs come into shops** from Stone tier.
9. **Furniture-seller exclusive items attract customers;** rarely a customer mentions the shop is missing a statue,
   and if there is one, they buy more.
10. **Decoration bar:** each decor object raises a "decoration liking %" inside the shop, hard to raise (to push buying
    decor); every item that gives decoration shows how much in its tooltip (plushies, shelves, statues, ...).

### Group E: implementation notes (after review)
- **Ranking** (`phone/Ranking.java`, app `client/phone/RankingApp.java`): capital = wallet + registers + shop tier value +
  assets (display stock value last seen by ShopLife, saved per shop as `stock_v`, and the market value of the cards in
  the player's General Binders, graded slabs at their graded value) - loan owed. Owners 11-100 have unique generated
  names. The app request is rate-limited (one every 2 s per player).
- **Diamond loan** (`phone/Loans.java`): the Diamond shop and the other rules are checked again when the offer is accepted.
- **Set completion reward** (`advancement/CardAdvancements.java`): only cards the player got from the game count
  (`CardCollection.pulled`: pack pulls and NPC gifts; old saves are seeded from `credited`), so a set passed between
  accounts pays once. Unpaid money (no CobbleDollars) is kept in `pending_set_money` and paid on a later binder action.
  The chat line has balls-only / money-only / pending variants.
- **Binder HUD** (`client/binder/GeneralBinderScreen.java`): «Closest to completion» and the reward are docked under the
  set list, always visible (falls back to the empty detail panel on very small screens).
- **Box-Opening Gloves** (`pack/BoxGloves.java`, `pack/PackOpener.openDirect`): opening whole boxes needs a General
  Binder (a Personal Binder holds 25 cards; the rest would spill on the ground); one broadcast per opening (best hit +
  how many more) instead of one per card.
- **Binders kept on death** (`binder/BinderKeep.java`): also the cursor and the 2x2 crafting grid; Accessories slots are
  emptied and refilled through the slot reference, and every binder goes back to its old slot. A death cancelled after
  our HEAD inject (NeoForge LivingDeathEvent, revive / totem mods) is detected at the end of the tick and the binders
  are handed back at once.
- Sounds are re-pitched/layered vanilla sound events (no original audio: there is no Vorbis encoder in the toolchain).
  Casino item icons use the 3D block models like every other machine.
