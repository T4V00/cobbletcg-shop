# MasterBall Traders Co.

**PTCG Shop Simulator & PvP**

> 🧩 **This is the Fabric version.** Playing on NeoForge? Get **[MasterBall Traders Co. (NeoForge)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/masterball-traders-co-neoforge)** instead. Use it with **[MasterBall Traders API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/masterball-traders-api)**.

### 🎉 Version 1.1: now on Fabric and NeoForge!

Open your own Pokémon card shop in Minecraft. Rip packs, chase hits, price your stock, haggle with customers, survive thieves and become the best TCG store in the server.

**MasterBall Traders Co.** is a Pokémon TCG shop simulator for Cobblemon worlds. Its cards come from **MasterBall Traders API** (11,700+ physical cards from 68 sets). Around that collection it builds a full trading-card economy: real market prices, a living shop full of NPC customers, pack openings that feel like the real thing, binders, grading, a phone full of apps, a casino, and real Pokémon TCG matches with a ranked league.

> ✅ **Compatible with COBBLEVERSE 1.8.0** (Cobblemon 1.8.4, Fabric, Minecraft 1.21.1). Just drop MasterBall Traders Co. and MasterBall Traders API into the modpack's `mods` folder.
>
> 🆕 **NeoForge 1.21.1 is supported since 1.1**, in its own project: see the table below.

## 1.1: what's new

- **NeoForge support.** Same features on Fabric and NeoForge, built from one code base.
- **Open / closed shop**, and **breaking your shop block keeps all progress** (money, stats, staff, reviews).
- **Staff:** cashiers walk up to customers, cashier upgrades (including one that handles hagglers), **up to 3 guards**, a clearer Upgrades tab.
- **Register:** withdraw by percentage with confirmation, and deposit money. Tips are capped at $50,000.
- **Customers:** a simpler haggling window, charm options (joke, compliment, story) that depend on Charisma, markup tolerance tied to reputation, 200+ Latin American names and 1,000+ new lines.
- **Cleanliness and decoration** matter much more: droppings to mop, graffiti to clean, a decoration bar, statues that boost sales, a 3-block-wide shutter and ten new decor objects.
- **New NPCs:** the **Mayor** collects taxes from Iron tier (refuse and face his Cobblemon team), robbed collectors fight back, the **CREATive Engineer** sells Create items, and Scalpers walk into shops.
- **Phone:** Grade 10 prices, a **Ranking app** to beat Don Cobbledome, owner of the Cobbledale Cobbledome, a $1,000,000 loan at Diamond tier, and calling the Furniture Seller.
- **Items and systems:** Box-Opening Gloves, binders that never drop on death, set-completion rewards, shelf markup, new gambling tables, sound effects and more frequent events.
- **Harder packs and grades:** much rarer hits and high grades, so a booster box only rarely pays for itself.

## 1.0 — the biggest update yet

**Thank you!** This mod started as a small idea and grew into a full shop simulator thanks to everyone who downloaded it, played it and left feedback. 1.0 is the result. If you enjoy it, a comment means a lot, and if something feels off, please tell us: **every comment, bug report and idea is read** and helps shape the next updates.

### Your shop
- **12 shop tiers**, from a tiny **Paper stall** (a 5×5 lemonade-stand shop you can craft with a diamond) to a 100×100 **Netherite** megastore. Upgrade in place by right-clicking your shop with a better core: you keep your stats, staff and reviews.
- **One shop per player** until you reach Amethyst; after that you can run a small chain.
- **A new shop menu** with **~100 missions**. The more you complete, the luckier you get with deals and tips.
- **Charisma:** a new level that makes your haggling more effective.
- **Tips:** customers who pay more than your price are tipping you, and they say so.
- **20 animated RGB displays** for the shop of your dreams.
- **Play tables need chairs** now, and customers may challenge you or sit down to play an exhibition match.
- **Statues** sometimes make visitors drop a pack nearby.

### People
- **New NPC look:** hundreds of generated skins with a 3D outer layer, men and women with real names.
- **10 unique customers** with rainbow names, their own personalities and dialogue. One is the best customer you'll ever have. One is a famous thief.
- **Thief tiers:** the more popular your shop, the bolder the thieves. Rare armed crews can take on your guards — so **upgrade your guards**.
- **Rob the Collector** if you dare: he turns into a mini-boss, and your reputation pays the price.

### The world
- **Trash cans** around villages: search them for packs and cards.
- **Bankrupt Card Shops:** abandoned, cobweb-covered stores near villages, with a "Permanently Closed" sign and something left in the trash.
- **The Wandering Duelist:** a pro player at a table in the wild. Beat him in a hard TCG match for 10,000… or lose your cash.
- **Casino:** slot machines, European roulette, blackjack, a fortune wheel and scratch cards, all with real-world odds.

### Phone
- **Messages app** with read/unread, delete and send.
- **Loans** from the Mysterious Wholesaler, with a credit history (pay on time!).
- **Stats app**, custom **wallpapers**, purchase notices, x36 booster boxes of every pack, and the TCG League from the second phone upgrade.
- **The phone in your hand is a real 3D phone** that shows your apps and changes with every upgrade. Tools are 3D models too.

### Collecting
- **Original card art by default.** On first launch the API asks whether you want to **download the official card art** (~3 GB, with a progress bar). Said no? Press **Y** (mod options) and click **Download official art** any time.
- God packs are always full art. The Mystery Box was reworked.
- Binder multi-select and a price filter per pack.

### New players
- Start with 5 packs and a personal binder already equipped. Your starting money arrives when you open your first pack.
- A mysterious collector, a very rude visitor and the Wholesaler will make sure your first days are interesting.

And many fixes: cards stuck in your hand, displays that refused cards, the manager goggles, unreadable notifications and more.

## Main features

**Your own card shop**
- Claim a shop plot and grow it through 12 tiers.
- Furnish it with multi-block displays, shelves, booster-box pallets, pedestals, graded-card walls and RGB showcases.
- Hire cashiers and an upgradeable guard.
- Set your prices, restock and watch customers walk in.

**Customers with personality**
- Regulars, picky buyers, rich collectors, hagglers and 10 unique characters browse, buy, tip and review you.
- Sellers bring you cards and packs, with their own personality, patience and reasons for selling.
- Thieves of every level try to run off with your stock.

**Pack opening, done right**
- Rip packs card by card with flips, holo shine, particles and god packs.
- Open 1 to 100 packs at once. Every pull shows its market value.

**Real market prices**
- Card, pack and box prices come from real TCGplayer market data and move with a dynamic daily market.

**The TCG Phone**
- Market with price charts, quick sell, shop management, messages, loans, stats, grading, the league, friends and their shops.

**Binders and collecting**
- A General Binder for your whole collection, customizable Personal Binders and a Binder Market.

**CobbleGrade**
- Send cards for grading (1.0 to 10) and display the slabs.

**Real Pokémon TCG matches**
- Official rules (60-card decks, prizes, evolutions, trainers) at play tables, against the AI or other players.
- A ranked league with 27 collectible 3D coins and 21 HD board backgrounds.

**Random events and reputation**
- 78 data-driven shop events, and a reputation that reacts to your shop's looks, value and safety.

**Fully configurable economy**
- Prices, wages, rewards and chances live in config files; reload them in-game with `/tcgshop reload`.

## How to install

1. Install Minecraft **1.21.1** with **Fabric Loader** (0.18.4 or newer). The CurseForge app does this for you with a Fabric profile.
2. Add **MasterBall Traders API**, **CobbleDollars**, **Fabric API** and **MasterBall Traders Co.** to your `mods` folder. Cobblemon is strongly recommended.
3. Create or open a world. You start with 5 packs and a binder.
4. Buy a **TCG Phone** from the Mysterious Wholesaler, a travelling merchant who shows up near players every now and then.
5. Press **Y** for the mod options (official art download, card art and more).

**On a server:** install the same mods on the server and on every client.
