# MasterBall Traders Co. without ScalperTCG

> **Done on 2026-11-22 (version 0.2.0 + MasterBall Traders API 0.1.0).** Cards and packs are API items
> (`masterball:*`), with downloadable images, our own pack contents and automatic conversion of the
> `cobbletcg:*` ids (item registry, Binder, collection, favourites, displays, CobbleGrade orders) and rescue of placed
> albums. ScalperTCG is marked as incompatible. What follows is the original plan, for reference.

Goal: make MasterBall Traders Co. work on its own, with its own cards and packs, using the images from the external
resource packs (which **MasterBall Traders API** will install) without shipping any image in the jar.

Inventory taken on 2026-11-22 against ScalperTCG 1.6.0 (`cobbletcg`).

What **no longer depends** on ScalperTCG: rarities, prices, CobbleGrade, TCG engine (twinleaf), species, shop and NPCs.
All card data is in `data/tcgshop/*` with `cobbletcg:...` ids; `tcgshop_rarity` already uses the key
without namespace (`<set>/<leaf>`).

## Phases

### 0. Our own data (done on 2026-11-22)
- `data/tcgshop/tcgshop_cards/catalog.json` (`tools/gen_card_catalog.py`): **72 sets, 12,572 cards** with name,
  printed number, set code and TCGplayer groups. Names and ids come from ScalperTCG 1.7.0 (CC0), used only
  as a reference: the jar is not installed and no asset is copied.
- `tools/fetch_rarities.py` now reads the catalog, not the jar: official rarity, official name and per-variant price for
  the 72 sets (11,644 of 11,766 physical TCG cards matched with TCGplayer). The 4 TCG Pocket sets
  (Genetic Apex, Mythical Island, Mega Rising, Crimson Blaze) are not on TCGplayer: rarity from ScalperTCG's tags
  and price from the median of their rarity.
- New sets compared to 1.6.0 (31): Jungle, Fossil, Base Set 2, Gym Heroes, Neo Genesis, Neo Discovery, Expedition,
  Aquapolis, Skyridge, EX Ruby & Sapphire, EX Sandstorm, Diamond & Pearl, Mysterious Treasures, Platinum, Rising Rivals,
  HeartGold & SoulSilver, Unleashed, Black & White, Emerging Powers, XY, Flashfire, Sun & Moon, Guardians Rising,
  Sword & Shield, Rebel Clash, Delta Reign (ME06), 30th Celebration and the 4 Pocket ones. All have images in
  packs 1-8 except **30th Celebration** (243 cards without an image yet).
- Careful: ScalperTCG's names are not reliable in every set (Perfect Order 89+ shifted in 1.7.0 too,
  Roaring Skies with offset ids). Use `tcg_name` from `tools/rarity_raw.json` and, if in doubt, look at the PNG.

### 1. Our own card and pack registry
- Register from `catalog.json` (official TCGplayer name when the catalog's doesn't match).
- Register `tcgshop:<set>/cards/<leaf>` and `tcgshop:<set>/sealed_<set>` / `riped2-4_`. Same path format, so
  `CardInfo` only changes `NS`.
- Translatable names with our own keys (`item.tcgshop.<set>.cards.<leaf>`) generated by script.
- Textures stay in `cobbletcg:texturas/cards/<set>/<leaf>.png` (the packs don't change).

### 2. Our own models and rendering
- A `ModelLoadingPlugin` that resolves any `/cards/` to our `card_template` (our own model, not copied) and
  a `BuiltinItemRenderer` that draws the pack's texture, with a default card if it is missing.
- Adjust `ShopRenderers.FRAME_CARD_H`/`frameCard` and `ItemRendererMixin` to the new geometry.

### 3. Our own pack opening
- Contents of each pack generated from our data (official rarity + the real pack structure of each era),
  not by copying its loot tables. `PackOpener` no longer needs `PackCapture` or the `offerOrDrop` mixin.
- Keep normal/semi-god/god (96/3/1) and add the print variants (already done in `PrintVariant`).

### 4. World migration
- Our own data with text ids (Binder, collection, Personal Binder, favourites): rewrite `cobbletcg:` →
  `tcgshop:` on load.
- Saved `ItemStack`s (displays, CobbleGrade orders, inventories, chests): a mixin that remaps unknown `cobbletcg:*`
  ids to `tcgshop:*` on decode.
- ScalperTCG albums with cards inside: convert their contents to our Binder the first time they are opened, or
  warn before removing ScalperTCG.

### 5. Albums, advancements and cleanup
- Replace the Panini album with our own (we already have the General and Personal Binders).
- Advancement icons to `tcgshop:`; remove `data/cobbletcg/*`, `fix_rarity_tags.py`, `fix_cobbletcg_advancements.py`.
- `fabric.mod.json`: remove `depends.cobbletcg` and declare `breaks`/conversion if both are installed.
- Repoint `tools/*.py` to our card list instead of the ScalperTCG jar.

### 6. Extra
- Packs 7 and 8 contain 30 sets that ScalperTCG 1.6.0 doesn't register (Jungle, Fossil, Neo, EX, DP, HGSS, BW, XY…,
  4,373 images). With our own registry we can add them by generating their data from TCGplayer.

## MasterBall Traders API (resource pack installer)
- Downloads the packs from your server the first time, checks their SHA-256 hash and puts them in `resourcepacks/`.
- Activates them automatically (what ScalperTCG does today with its mixin).
- Shows progress on the title screen, allows resuming downloads and doesn't block the game if there is no connection.
- Note: the card images are owned by The Pokémon Company / Nintendo / Creatures / Game Freak and their
  illustrators. Hosting them yourself is where the legal risk is (DMCA): the API should only download, and the
  hosting and responsibility should be clear.
